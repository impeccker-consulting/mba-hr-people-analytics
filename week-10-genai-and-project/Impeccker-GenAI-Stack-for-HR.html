Under the hood

How AI really works, in plain words

The 30-second truth

An AI model is a very well-read autocomplete. It has read billions of pages. When you type, it picks the most likely next word, again and again. It does not look things up, it forgets everything between chats, and it cannot press any button. Everything in this course is a way of working around those facts.

Why five layers? Each one fixes one problem Big picture

1 · PromptA vague request gets a vague answer. Five recruiters ask for a job description and get five different, generic ones. 2 · ContextThe AI has never seen your leave policy, so it guesses what “most companies” do, or quotes an outdated one. 3 · AgentA chatbot can advise on scheduling 12 interviews, but someone still has to do every step. An agent does them. 4 · HarnessOnce AI can act, one wrong move is real: an offer far above the pay band, or Aadhaar numbers emailed outside the company. 5 · LoopNobody checks the AI’s shortlist, so a hidden bias (such as favouring top colleges) goes unnoticed until someone complains.

Demo 1: the AI is guessing the next word Try it

Ask: “Our notice period for a Store Manager is…”. Watch how likely each next word is.

Demo 2: AI reads in pieces called “tokens” Type anything

Why this matters for cost: you pay per token, both for what you send and what comes back. Roughly ₹0.3 to ₹2 per 1,000 words (indicative). Pasting a 400-page handbook (about 200,000 words) with every question costs about ₹60 per question. Sending just the 3 right pages costs about ₹0.5. That is why Layer 2 saves money.

Jargon buster

ModelThe AI “brain” (e.g. Claude, GPT). Trained once on huge text.
TokenA small piece of a word. The unit AI reads and bills in.
Context windowThe AI’s desk. Everything it can see right now.
HallucinationA confident answer that is simply made up.
RAGA librarian step: fetch the right pages first, then answer.
Tool callThe AI asking a program to do something, like book a slot.
GuardrailA fixed rule that stops unsafe actions.
EvalA test that scores how good the AI’s output is.

How HR itself changes

The HR function: As Is → To Be

The big idea

AI does not remove HR. It removes the admin inside HR so people can spend time on what only humans do well: advice, empathy, judgement and trust. The headcount may stay similar. What changes is the mix of work, roles and skills. Drag the slider, or press Transform, and watch one HR team change.

3 · The shape of the HR team (mix of roles, not just numbers)

The shape moves from a wide admin base toward a diamond: fewer pure-admin roles, more advisors and a few new AI-oversight roles. Most people are reskilled and redeployed, not removed.
